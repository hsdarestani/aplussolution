# Master-data quick access

The `Personal & Kunden` page exposes a mobile-first quick panel for `Einsatzorte` and `Positionen`.

Expected UX:

- Newly created records sort to the top immediately.
- Search filters both locations and positions.
- `Alle anzeigen` expands long lists without forcing users to hunt through the page.
- Position colors are selected with a native color picker; no HEX knowledge is required.
- Records can be edited and activated/deactivated without deleting historical references.
